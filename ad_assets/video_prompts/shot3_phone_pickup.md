# Video Prompt — Shot 3 (Phone Pickup, Game Opens)

**时长**: 3 秒
**起始关键帧**: `prompts/shot3_phone_pickup.md`
**镜头风格**: 特写转场镜头

---

## 视频生成 Prompt

```
Anime cel-shaded animation, transition shot.

The boy sits cross-legged on the grass, his head hanging slightly. He pulls
a smartphone from his pocket, holds it horizontally with both hands. The
camera dollies in close to focus on the phone screen.

On the phone screen, a 2D top-down disc golf game UI appears: green grass
field, a yellow disc on a tee pad on the left, a metal basket on the right,
cyan trajectory preview line, "POWER" meter at the bottom, distance rings
around the basket. The game UI elements animate in subtly (HUD fades in,
trajectory line draws itself).

The boy's eyes widen with curiosity as the game loads.

Camera: starts on the boy's slumped posture, dollies down to his phone screen.
At 2 seconds, the camera is locked on the phone screen filling the frame.

Lighting: ambient outdoor daylight, slight bloom on the phone screen to draw
attention.

Mood: shift from defeat to curiosity. The phone screen feels inviting.

Duration: 3 seconds.
Style: anime cel-shading transitioning into vivid 2D game UI screenshot art.
The transition should feel natural — the phone screen is a window INTO the game.
```

---

## 摄影机运动

1. **0-1.5s**: 中景男孩坐草地，掏手机
2. **1.5-2.5s**: 推近到手机屏幕特写
3. **2.5-3s**: 屏幕完全填满画面，UI 元素动画进入

## 关键效果

- 手机屏幕**画面必须清楚显示游戏 UI** —— 这是整个广告的"sell"瞬间
- 屏幕上的 UI 元素一个个 fade in（POWER 条、轨迹线、HUD）
- 屏幕亮度高于环境，自动吸引视线

## 转场到下一镜

- 手机屏幕填满画面后，**直接 cut 到 Shot 4 游戏内画面**（无缝过渡）
- 视觉上手机屏幕里的游戏画面"成为整个画面"
