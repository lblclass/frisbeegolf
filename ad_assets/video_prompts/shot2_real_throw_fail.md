# Video Prompt — Shot 2 (Real Throw + Tree Hit)

**时长**: 4 秒
**起始关键帧**: `prompts/shot2_real_throw_fail.md` 的两个 keyframes
**镜头风格**: 动作镜头，撞击瞬间慢动作

---

## 视频生成 Prompt

```
Anime cel-shaded animation, sports manga action sequence.

The teenage boy stands confidently on a concrete tee pad in a wide grassland.
He winds up his right arm with intense focus, then explodes forward in a
powerful backhand throw. The yellow frisbee disc launches from his hand,
trailing visible motion lines as it speeds forward across the field.

Camera follows the disc's flight in a slow tracking shot.

At 2.5 seconds, the disc smashes directly into a large oak tree trunk.
Time slows to slow-motion for the impact: bark chips fly outward, cartoon
impact stars burst around the collision point, exaggerated speed lines
radiate from the tree.

The disc bounces off and falls to the ground.

Cut to the boy: his expression collapses from confident to shocked then frustrated,
he throws his arms up in disbelief, then slumps his shoulders.

Camera: starts behind boy looking forward, tracks disc forward, then whip-pans
back to boy after impact.

Lighting: bright midday sun, hard shadows.

Mood: confident → shock → frustrated. Dramatic, exaggerated, comedic timing.

Duration: 4 seconds.
Style: clean anime cel-shading, sports manga energy, comic impact effects.
```

---

## 摄影机运动

1. **0-1s**: 后跟拍男孩出手动作
2. **1-2.5s**: 跟踪飞盘飞行（左→右画过）
3. **2.5-3s**: **撞树！慢动作**（time stretch）
4. **3-4s**: 回切男孩沮丧表情

## 关键效果

- **撞击慢动作**是这一镜的灵魂，让观众"哎呀"
- 飞盘要画速度线（漫画风格）
- 撞击星爆 + 树皮碎屑

## 转场到下一镜

- 沮丧表情停留 0.5 秒 → 切到 Shot 3（坐草地）
