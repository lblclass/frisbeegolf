# Video Prompt — Shot 5 (Redemption Throw)

**时长**: 4 秒
**起始关键帧**: `prompts/shot5_redemption.md`
**镜头风格**: 跟拍 + 慢动作出手

---

## 视频生成 Prompt

```
Anime cel-shaded animation, sports manga climax shot.

The teenage boy stands on the tee pad in a wide open landscape resembling a
desert plain with scattered green oases. The sunset behind him bathes
everything in warm orange light. His hair and shirt ripple in a gentle breeze.

He looks down at his hand briefly with a confident smile, then assumes a
relaxed throwing stance. With a fluid, controlled motion he releases the disc
in a clean backhand throw. The yellow disc leaves his hand smoothly with a
subtle cyan trail — the same cyan as the game's trajectory line.

Slow-motion at the moment of release: the disc spinning visibly, his
follow-through perfect.

Then: time resumes normal speed. The disc soars across the landscape toward
the distant basket.

Camera: side tracking shot moving with him as he throws, then continues
tracking the disc as it flies away.

Lighting: golden hour rim-lit silhouette, deep orange sunset gradient sky.

Mood: confident, in control, redemption. Stark contrast to Shot 2's failed throw.

Duration: 4 seconds.
Style: cinematic anime, sports manga finale energy, golden hour color grading.
Inspired by Slam Dunk's iconic shot release sequences.
```

---

## 摄影机运动

1. **0-1s**: 侧跟拍男孩，他低头自信微笑
2. **1-2.5s**: 跟拍他出手动作 + **慢动作释放飞盘**
3. **2.5-4s**: 时间恢复正常，相机跟随飞盘飞向远方

## 关键效果

- **慢动作出手**和 Shot 2 的撞击慢动作形成对应
- **青色尾迹**是关键 callback —— 让观众潜意识联想"游戏练习生效了"
- Shot 2 的撞树音 vs Shot 5 的优雅释放，**情绪对比**
