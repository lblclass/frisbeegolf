# Video Prompt — Shot 4 (Game Gameplay Montage)

**时长**: 5 秒
**起始关键帧**: `prompts/shot4_game_play.md` 的 3 个 keyframes
**镜头风格**: 跟随飞盘的连续动作镜头

---

## 视频生成 Prompt

```
Top-down 2D game animation, mobile sports game aesthetic, flat shaded vector art.

A yellow disc launches from a tee pad, leaving a glowing cyan flight trail
behind it. The camera tracks the disc's flight across three connected scenes:

PART 1 (0-1.7s) — FOREST: The disc curves through a dense forest of dark
green tree circles. It gracefully avoids the trees with a clean S-curve.
HUD shows "Stroke 2 • Mid • Hyzer +0.4". The basket is visible in the corner.

PART 2 (1.7-3.4s) — LAKE: The scene transitions smoothly. The disc soars
over a deep blue lake with small white wave ripples. A small fairway island
sits in the middle. Wind direction arrow visible in HUD. The disc just barely
clears the water and lands on the far shore.

PART 3 (3.4-5s) — HARD GROUND: Final transition to a grey concrete approach.
The disc descends in a clean arc toward a metal basket. As it approaches,
distance rings around the basket pulse. Small golden sparkle particles begin
to appear around the basket in anticipation.

Camera: smooth tracking shot following the disc throughout, maintaining
similar framing distance, never cutting away.

Lighting: flat illumination, vivid saturated colors.

Mood: flow state, the player is in the zone, satisfying smooth gameplay.

Duration: 5 seconds.
Style: top-down 2D game screenshot art. Flat shaded, NOT 3D, NOT photorealistic.
Clean readable UI. Inspired by Mini Motorways, Alto's Odyssey, modern indie
mobile games.
```

---

## 摄影机运动

- 整段都是跟随飞盘的 tracking shot，**永远不切换镜头**
- 三个场景之间用**地表渐变**（草地→水面→水泥）自然过渡
- 飞盘和轨迹线保持画面焦点

## 关键效果

- **青色轨迹线**贯穿整段（关键视觉符号）
- HUD 元素**保持可见**（让观众明白这是游戏）
- 三种地表**颜色对比鲜明**（深绿森林→蓝色湖泊→灰色硬地）
- 不要切镜头！整段是一个连续 tracking shot

## 转场到下一镜

- 在硬地段末尾，飞盘即将进筐时，**画面亮度爆增**（白色闪光）
- 直接 cut 到 Shot 5（男孩抬头从手机看回现实）
- 视觉对比：游戏画面（鲜艳扁平）→ 现实画面（写实金色）
