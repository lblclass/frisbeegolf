# Video Prompt — Shot 1 (Locker Room)

**时长**: 4 秒
**起始关键帧**: `prompts/shot1_locker.md` 生成的图像
**镜头风格**: 静态机位，轻微推镜

---

## 视频生成 Prompt

```
Anime cel-shaded animation, sports manga style.

The teenage Asian boy sits on a wooden bench in a sunlit locker room,
focused on tying his red sneakers. He pulls the laces tight with both hands,
glances down at the yellow frisbee tucked under his arm, then looks up directly
at the camera with a determined smile. He stands up, slings the black duffel
bag over his shoulder, and steps off-frame to the right.

Camera: starts at medium shot, slow push-in over 3 seconds to a medium close-up
as he stands up. Smooth tripod-locked, no shake.

Lighting: warm golden hour light from the right window, slow visible dust
particles drifting in the light beam.

Mood: focused, determined, calm before action.

Duration: 4 seconds.
Style: clean cel-shaded anime, hard shadows, no painterly textures.
Inspired by Haikyuu, Slam Dunk, Ao Ashi sports manga aesthetic.
```

---

## 摄影机运动

1. **0-2s**: 静止医镜头，男孩低头系鞋带
2. **2-3s**: 缓慢推近到中近景
3. **3-4s**: 男孩抬头看镜头微笑，站起来挎包

## 转场到下一镜

- 男孩走出画面右侧 → 直接 cut 到 Shot 2 草原镜头
- 或：用挎包动作做 match-cut（包甩到肩 → 切到草原远景）

## 备注

- 没有对话，纯视觉
- 配乐建议：低音鼓 + 滴答声铺垫紧张感
