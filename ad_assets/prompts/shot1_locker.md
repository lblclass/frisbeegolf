# Shot 1 — Locker Room Preparation

**时长**: 4 秒
**情绪**: 期待、准备
**关键画面**: 漫画男孩在更衣室穿运动装备，挎飞盘包

---

## 图像生成 Prompt（用于生成 keyframe）

```
Anime cel-shaded illustration, comic book style with bold clean lineart,
a teenage Asian boy with messy black hair and bright determined eyes,
sitting on a wooden bench in a sunlit locker room,
tying the laces of his red sneakers in a forward-leaning pose,
wearing a white athletic t-shirt with red trim and black athletic shorts,
a yellow frisbee disc tucked under his arm,
a black sports duffel bag on the bench beside him,
warm golden hour light streaming through a window from the right,
soft volumetric light rays, dust particles visible in the light,
locker room background slightly blurred with bokeh,
camera angle: medium shot from the front, slight low angle to feel heroic,
color palette: warm yellows, deep reds, muted browns, with cool blue shadows,
high contrast, sharp focus on character,
inspired by the style of Haikyuu, Slam Dunk, Ao Ashi,
clean professional anime production quality, 16:9 aspect ratio
```

---

## 风格关键词（短版，备用）

- anime / manga style
- cel-shaded, hard shadows
- clean lineart, no painterly textures
- warm color palette, golden hour
- determined teen athlete
- sports manga aesthetic

- 单一定焦镜头，slight low angle（仰角约 10°）让角色更有英雄感

- 浅景深，背景虚化
