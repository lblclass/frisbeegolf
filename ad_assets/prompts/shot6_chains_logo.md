**情绪**: 胜利、释放
**关键画面**: 飞盘进筐链子声 + 游戏 LOGO + "Out Now"

```
Anime cel-shaded illustration, comic book style,
extreme close-up of a yellow frisbee disc colliding with metal chains of a disc golf basket,
the chains visibly bouncing and dispersing the disc's energy,
cartoon-style impact stars and motion lines around the chain hit,
metal chain rings rendered with detailed metallic shading,
the disc tilting slightly as it falls into the basket cage below,
slow-motion frozen frame energy,
small confetti particles and yellow celebration sparkles bursting outward,
dramatic backlighting with sun behind the basket creating a halo,
camera angle: extreme close-up at chain level, slightly low,
color palette: silver chains, bright yellow disc, warm orange sunset backdrop,
high contrast, cinematic action moment,
sports manga finale aesthetic,
16:9 aspect ratio
```

Clean minimal title card design,

- 进筐瞬间用 slow-mo 效果，让链子撞击声有时间发出
- 进筐后立即过渡到 logo card，干净利落
- Logo 颜色保持游戏一致：深绿背景 + 黄色文字 + 青色高光
- CTA 文字（"Out Now"）可以根据实际发行渠道改
